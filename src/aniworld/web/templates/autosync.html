Auto-Sync

How it works

  1. Tick "Sync this with Auto-Sync" in the download dialog of a series. It syncs in the language and into the download folder picked there.
  2. Pick the episode it starts at. Nothing before it is ever queued, so gaps you leave on purpose stay gaps.
  3. Pick when it is checked: on the Auto-Sync schedule from the settings, every day, or once a week, each at a time of your choosing.
  4. A check reads the series page and queues every episode from the start point on that is out in that language and not in the folder yet.

Only series you ticked are synced. aniworld.to and SerienStream titles can be synced.
